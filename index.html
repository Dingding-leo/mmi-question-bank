How to prepare for an MMI

Understand the format

An MMI is a circuit of 6–10 timed stations (2 min prep, 5–8 min response). Each tests a different attribute — communication, ethics, teamwork, resilience. You're scored per station.

Use the PAUSE structure

Pause, identify the core demand, list your key points, then act. This universal scaffold works on any station you're handed on the day.

Practise aloud, not silently

Read the scenario, then answer out loud for 60–90 seconds. Record yourself. Rehearsing aloud is what builds the real skill.

Drill role-plays with a partner

Communication stations are the most commonly failed. Run them live with a partner playing the patient, and have them ask the follow-ups.
